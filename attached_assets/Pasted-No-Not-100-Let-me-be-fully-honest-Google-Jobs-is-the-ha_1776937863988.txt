**What makes it unreliable:**

Google actively fights scrapers with multiple layers:

1. **Bot detection (Cloudflare/reCAPTCHA)** — Even with proxy + real user-agent, Google detects headless Chrome via JavaScript fingerprinting. It checks things like: canvas rendering, WebGL, fonts installed, mouse movement patterns. Headless Chrome fails these checks silently and just shows a CAPTCHA or blank page instead of jobs.

2. **CSS selectors break** — Google changes class names like `.iFjolb`, `.BjJfJf` randomly (sometimes weekly). Your scraper breaks with zero warning. You have to re-inspect and fix manually.

3. **IP bans** — Even residential proxies get flagged if you hit Google too fast or too many times from the same proxy pool.

4. **Dynamic loading** — Google Jobs loads results inside a JavaScript-rendered panel that requires scrolling. Miss the scroll timing and you get 0 results.
